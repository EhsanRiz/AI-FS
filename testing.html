FS Field Monitoring — Testing Guide

AI-Powered Extension for Agricultural Resilience · Lesotho

Open the app → fs.4dcs.co.za

1 · Install the app on your phone

  1. Open fs.4dcs.co.za in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone).
  2. Open the browser menu (⋮ on Android, the share square on iPhone).
  3. Choose “Add to Home Screen”, then Add / Install.
  4. Open it from your home screen — it runs full-screen with the 4D logo.
The app works without signal once installed — everything you capture is saved on the phone and sent automatically when you have network again.

2 · Sign in

Use your first name and your PIN (sent to you privately — never share it).

3 · How the test runs

1
Office walkthrough (1–2 days)
Programme staff + 2–3 Field Supervisors go through the checklists below together, phones in hand.
2
Field pilot (2–3 days)
The same Field Supervisors do real practice visits at their own stations — including one full day in aeroplane mode.
3
Go-live
After sign-off all practice data is wiped, so real monitoring starts from zero. Practise freely — nothing you enter now is permanent.
